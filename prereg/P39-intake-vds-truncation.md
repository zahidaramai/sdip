## Disclosure (what was seen before this registration)

Exploratory runs on 2026-11-17, on F3 copies regenerated with `prereg/P38_make_vds.py`, chose
the method: `openvds.open` and lazy page reads show nothing for a cut of 1,000 bytes; a cut at
20 kB opens and returns all-zero amplitudes; `HueBDS check` exits 1 on cuts inside the index
pages and, on the 1,000-byte cut, exits 0 but maps an extent 1,000 bytes past the end of the
file. The cut rule and gates below are fixed now; the measured run is the one after this commit.
